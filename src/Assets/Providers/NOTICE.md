# Provider marks

These are the providers' identifying marks, not invented application glyphs. The marks belong to their respective owners and identify only the corresponding usage widgets.

- Claude: the Claude asterisk, orange #D97757. SVG distribution: https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons/blob/master/packages/static-svg/icons/claude-color.svg . Branding reference: https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/manage-claude/oauth-apps/branding-samples . Icon-library license: https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons/blob/master/LICENSE .
- Codex/OpenAI: OpenAI blossom. SVG distribution: https://github.com/anomalyco/models.dev/blob/dev/providers/openai/logo.svg . Branding reference: https://openai.com/brand/ .
- Gemini: Google's original sparkle SVG: https://www.gstatic.com/lamda/images/gemini_sparkle_v002_d4735304ff6292a690345.svg . Original gradient is retained.
- OpenCode: original mark distributed by its own organization: https://github.com/anomalyco/models.dev/blob/dev/providers/opencode/logo.svg .

SVG shapes, proportions, clear space and colored fills are retained. Monochrome currentColor marks follow the interface's text color for legibility. No runtime downloads or icon service are used. The source SVGs are embedded in the application.
